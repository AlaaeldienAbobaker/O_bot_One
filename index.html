This policy explains how the O Bot Discord application (“the Bot”) handles data. The Bot is operated by our community staff for servers we administer. It assigns and maintains community roles, attributes invites, and posts staff logs inside Discord.

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use Discord data to train machine learning or AI models.

What we collect

  • Discord user IDs
  • Wallet addresses that a user chooses to verify
  • Invite statistics (joins, leaves, and related timestamps)
  • Guild IDs, role IDs, and channel IDs needed to operate the Bot

What we do not collect

  • Email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, or payment cards
  • Direct messages
  • Message content stored outside Discord
  • Precise location or advertising identifiers

How we use the data

We use this data only to:

  • Assign and remove community roles
  • Attribute invites and grant invite-reward roles
  • Provide staff join, leave, and role-change logs inside Discord

Wallet addresses that a user chooses to verify are sent to Solana RPC providers so the Bot can confirm ownership and check token balances. We do not sell data or share it with advertisers. We do not use Discord data to train machine learning or AI models.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on servers we control, in application files used by the Bot. Access is limited to operators of the Bot. We keep records only while they are needed to keep roles and invite rewards accurate, or until a deletion request is completed.

Your choices

  • Do not verify a wallet if you do not want a wallet address stored
  • Leave the server to stop further join and leave processing
  • Contact a server administrator to request deletion of stored user ID, wallet, or invite records

Contact

To request access, correction, or deletion, contact a server administrator in the Discord server where O Bot is installed.
